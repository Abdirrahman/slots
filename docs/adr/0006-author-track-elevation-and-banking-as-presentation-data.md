# Author Track elevation and banking as presentation data

Authored Tracks will carry height and bank on every presentation path point, and the renderer will sample a Track surface with elevation, pitch, and roll while the authoritative simulation keeps using only progress and Lane. This gives the Workshop circuit its flyover, climbs, and banked turns without adding physics, and lets a Track cross over itself because two Cars at the same bench position are separated by authored height rather than by collision geometry. The cost is that presentation and simulation now disagree about shape: a banked or climbing section covers more bench distance than its progress suggests, so layouts must keep a minimum turn radius — inner and outer Lanes of a tight corner otherwise travel visibly different distances at the same progress speed.
